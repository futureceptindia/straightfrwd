Shiv Group has worked with soya since before most protein brands in India existed. StraightFrwd is the group’s first pack of its own.

Why we exist

India does not eat enough protein, and the shelf is not helping.

Most of what is sold as protein in this country is either priced for a gym membership or written for someone who already knows the vocabulary. Turn the tub around and the number on the front stops making sense.

We have spent over twenty years making the raw material. StraightFrwd is us making the finished thing, so that filling a protein gap costs one sachet, one spoon and thirty seconds.

The origin

A manufacturer, first.

StraightFrwd is a brand of Shiv Protein Private Limited, part of Shiv Group.

The group has been in Indian manufacturing for over forty years. Twenty of those have gone into one ingredient.

One ingredient, two decades

The miracle seed.

Soya has been the centre of the work for over twenty years. People here call it the miracle seed.

Twenty years on one ingredient teaches you things breadth does not. How the seed behaves after a poor crop year. Which process steps change the product and which only look like they do. A long memory of what went wrong, held by the people who were standing there when it did.

From humble beginnings to India’s premier soya isolate producer, built on hardwork and innovation.

Where it is made

In our own facility, in India.

Own research labs. The group runs the plants itself.

What we hold to

Three things, and we do not move on them.

01

Nature and science

Soya is a plant. What we do to it happens in our own labs, with instruments and people who read the output. Natural nutrition, validated by measurement.

02

Everyday ease

A sachet, a spoon, a glass of plain water. No shaker, no milk, no blender. Nutrition only works if you actually take it, and anything that needs planning gets abandoned by the second week.

03

The long game

Nobody fixes their protein intake in a fortnight. We would rather you drank this for ten years than took it seriously for ten days.
