About

We got tired of
reading the back.

Straightfrwd started with a shelf in a Delhi chemist's shop and eleven tins of protein, none of which would tell you plainly what was in them.

Every tin had a big number on the front. Turn it around and the number stopped making sense — blends with names but no amounts, fillers that pushed the scoop size up, sweeteners nobody wanted to admit to.

So the brief for our first product was small and annoying to meet. Put the whole formula on the pack. Name every enzyme and every mineral. Print the sweetener on the front. Make the serving 50 g and make 22.5 g of that protein, so the maths works without a calculator.

That is the whole company. There is no origin story about a marathon.

What we hold to

Four things, and we don't move on them.

Nothing unnamed

If it is in the mix, it is on the pack with its INS number. No "proprietary blend", no "herbal complex".

Water, not marketing

It is built to mix in plain water with a spoon. If a product needs a ₹900 shaker to be drinkable, the product is the problem.

Say what it isn't

This is not a meal replacement and it does not treat anything. That line is on the box because it is true, not because a regulator made us.

Short catalogue

Two products. Seven flavours. We will add an eighth when there is a reason, not when the quarter needs one.
