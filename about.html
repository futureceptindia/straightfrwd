About

Four decades with
one seed.

Shiv Protein Private Limited is the newest company in the Shiv Group. The group has spent over forty years building one business, and more than twenty of those years working with soya.

Most of that time was spent on the unglamorous end of the business. Sourcing the seed, refining it, and getting soya isolate to a standard that other manufacturers would buy and put their own name on. That work made Shiv Group India's premier producer of soya isolate.

StraightFrwd is the first time the group has put its own name on the front of a pack. The reason is simple enough: most Indians do not get the protein they need in a day, and the products meant to close that gap are either priced out of reach or impossible to understand.

So we are starting with four products, made in our own facility, from the ingredient we already know better than anyone in the country.

How we think about it

Nature and science, in the same tin.

Three ideas sit behind everything we make. They are not slogans — they decide what goes in and what stays out.

One

Nature + science

The seed is grown, not synthesised. What we do to it afterwards is laboratory work — refining, testing, checking each batch. Both halves matter, and we would not trust a product that skipped either.

Two

Everyday ease

A sachet you can carry, a spoon, a glass of water. Nutrition only works if you actually take it, and anything that needs planning gets abandoned by the second week.

Three

The long game

Nobody fixes their health in a month. We would rather you drank this for ten years than took it seriously for ten days, which is why it is priced and flavoured to be ordinary.

Where it is made

Our own floor.

Soya isolate has been the group's business for two decades, so the infrastructure and the research labs were already here before StraightFrwd existed. We are not renting a production slot from someone else and putting a label on the output.

Every batch is made in India, tested in our labs, and carries the licence numbers on the pack.
