Notes

01, 03, 08 are solid and read as primary actions. Only one of these should exist per screen, otherwise nothing looks primary.

02, 09 are the natural secondary pair to whichever solid one is chosen, so a primary and secondary can be picked from the same row.

04, 07 are tertiary. Good on cards, too quiet for a hero.

05 has an angled tail, so it cannot sit flush in a grid without the point colliding with whatever is to its right. Needs clearance.

06 is the only one that does not use the brand radius. It will look like a different design system next to the current cards unless the radius is reconciled.
