Questions

Before you ask.

Is this a meal replacement?

No. It is a daily protein mix. The pack says so plainly — it is not meant to treat, cure or prevent anything, and it should not stand in for a meal.

Whey or plant?

Plant. Soy protein isolate, pea protein and brown rice protein. Nothing dairy-derived, so it is vegetarian and it suits people whey does not sit well with.

Why no added sugar?

It is sweetened with sucralose and acesulfame potassium instead, which is why a 50 g serving still comes in at 200 kcal. Both are declared on the front of the pack.

Water or milk?

Water. The mix carries soy fat powder, so it goes creamy on its own. Milk works if you want it richer, but you are adding calories the label does not account for.

Who should not take it?

Pregnant and nursing women, children under five, and anyone on medical supervision, unless a doctor or a certified dietitian tells you otherwise. Contains soy; made in a facility that also processes wheat, milk, oats and nuts.
