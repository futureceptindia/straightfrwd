Questions

Before you ask.

Is this a meal replacement?

Yes — the pack says "recommended as a one meal replacement". One serving a day, or as directed by a physician, certified dietitian or nutritional professional. It is not intended to treat, cure, mitigate or prevent any disease.

Whey or plant?

Plant — soya protein isolate, pea protein and brown rice protein. One caveat worth knowing: the soy fat powder contains sodium caseinate, which is milk-derived, so it is not dairy-free.

Why no added sugar?

It is sweetened with sucralose and acesulfame potassium instead, which is why a 50 g serving still comes in at 200 kcal. Both are declared on the front of the pack.

Water or milk?

Water. The mix carries soy fat powder, so it goes creamy on its own. Milk works if you want it richer, but you are adding calories the label does not account for.

Who should not take it?

Pregnant, nursing and lactating women, children under 5, adolescents and the elderly, unless a physician or certified dietitian advises it. Contains soy; made in a facility that also processes wheat, milk, oats and nuts.
