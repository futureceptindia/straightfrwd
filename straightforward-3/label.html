The label.

Everything printed on the back of a Daily Protein pack, in the order it is printed, grouped so it reads in a minute.

As printed on the pack

Recommended use.

One serving a day, or as directed by a physician, certified dietitian or nutritional professional. Do not exceed the recommended daily usage. Recommended as a one meal replacement.

Not for.

Pregnant, nursing and lactating women, children under 5, adolescents or the elderly, except when medically advised by a physician, certified dietitian or nutrition professional.

How to make it.

Add 50 g of powder to 250 ml of water and stir until it has completely dissolved. Drink it straight away.

The small print.

Not intended to treat, cure, mitigate or prevent any specific disease, disorder or condition. For oral consumption only.
