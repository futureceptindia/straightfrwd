      Before you ask.

      Is it a meal replacement?

      Yes. The pack says "recommended as a one meal replacement". One serving a day, or as directed by a physician, certified dietitian or nutrition professional. It is not meant to treat, cure, mitigate or prevent any disease.

      Whey or plant?

      Plant. Soya protein isolate, pea protein and brown rice protein. The soy fat powder contains sodium caseinate, which comes from milk, so it is not dairy-free.

      How long does a 1 kg pouch last?

      A 1 kg pouch holds 20 servings of 50 g. Move the slider to see how long that is for you.

      About 20 days

      Water or milk?

      Water. The mix carries its own fat powder and goes creamy on its own. Milk works if you want it richer, but it adds calories the label does not count.

      Who should not take it?

      Pregnant, nursing and lactating women, children under 5, adolescents and the elderly, unless a physician or certified dietitian advises it. Contains soy. Made in a facility that also processes wheat, milk, oats and nuts.

      How do I make the herbal drinks?

      One 1 g sachet in 150 ml of hot water. Stir and drink. Kashmiri Kahwa, Ginger, Tulsi and Lemon.
