For over twenty years, we’ve had a front-row seat to Indian nutrition. Before StraightFrwd ever existed on a shelf, our parent company, Shiv Group, spent two decades quietly mastering soya protein isolate, growing from humble beginnings into one of India’s premier producers.

Now, after four decades in Indian manufacturing and twenty years of working with soya, we are putting our own name on the front of the pack for the first time.

We created StraightFrwd with a single goal: to help Indian households close their daily nutrition gap without overcomplicating their lives.

Why we started

Closing the gap, building the trust.

India has a well-documented dietary protein gap. Our everyday meals, rich and comforting as they are, often fall short of the protein and micronutrients our bodies need for the long run.

At the same time, walking down the supplement aisle can feel overwhelming. Between intimidating jargon, overpromised results, and a very real industry trust deficit around quality and adulteration, taking care of your family’s nutrition has become harder than it needs to be.

We believe you shouldn’t need a degree in nutrition to feed yourself and your family well. And you certainly shouldn’t have to second-guess what is inside your pack.

StraightFrwd was born to offer the exact opposite of confusion: clear labels, familiar Indian flavours, effortless formats, and complete transparency about what goes into our products and who makes them.

How we think about your health

Three simple principles.

01

Nature + science, working together

We start with plant-based foundations and traditional botanicals, and back them with nutritional science.

02

Everyday ease

The healthiest habit is the one you actually keep.
