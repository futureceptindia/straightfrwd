The product

What is Daily Protein?

A plant protein mix you stir into water. One 50 g serving gives you 22.5 g of protein and 4.4 g of dietary fibre, plus vitamins, minerals and digestive enzymes. It comes as a 50 g sachet and a 1 kg pack.

Whey or plant?

Plant — soya protein isolate, pea protein and brown rice protein. One caveat worth knowing: the soy fat powder contains sodium caseinate, which is milk-derived, so it is not dairy-free.

How much protein and fibre, exactly?

Per 50 g serving: 22.5 g protein and 4.4 g dietary fibre, of which 1 g is FOS. Per 100 g that is 45 g protein and 8.8 g fibre. The full panel is on What’s Inside.

Is there sugar in it?

No added sugar. Total sugars are 1.12 g in a serving, and it is sweetened with sucralose (INS 955) and acesulfame potassium (INS 950). Both are named on the front of the pack.

Do all three flavours have the same nutrition?

Yes. Chocolate, Mango and Kulfi share one formulation — the same ingredient list and the same nutrition panel. Only the flavour changes.

Using it

How do I make it?

Add 50 g of powder to 250 mL of water and stir well until the powder has completely dissolved. Drink it immediately after preparation.

Water or milk?

The pack says water. The mix carries its own soy fat powder, so it goes creamy on its own. Milk works if you want it richer, but you are adding calories the panel does not account for.

How many servings a day?

One, or as directed by a physician, certified dietitian or nutritional professional. Do not exceed the recommended daily usage.

Is this a meal replacement?

Yes — the pack says “recommended as a one meal replacement”. It is not intended to treat, cure, mitigate or prevent any disease, disorder or condition.

How many servings in a 1 kg pack?

Twenty, at 50 g each.

How do I make the herbal drinks?

One 1 g sachet in 150 ml of hot water. Four flavours: Kashmiri Kahwa, Ginger, Tulsi and Lemon.

Safety and allergens

What are the allergens?

Contains soy. Manufactured in a facility which also processes wheat, milk, oats and nuts. The soy fat powder also contains sodium caseinate, which comes from milk.

Who should not take it?

It is not to be used by pregnant, nursing and lactating women, children under 5, adolescents or the elderly, except when medically advised by a physician, certified dietitian or nutrition professional.

Is it vegetarian?

The pack carries the green vegetarian mark. It is a plant protein formulation, though as noted above it is not dairy-free.

How should I store it?

In a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and moisture, out of reach of children. Close the container tightly after every use.
