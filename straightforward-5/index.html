How to make

Under a minute. No blender.

  1. 01

    Tear

    Open one 50 g sachet. On a pouch, that is one of its 20 servings.

  2. 02

    Pour

    Add it to 250 ml of water.

  3. 03

    Stir

    Mix well with a spoon or a shaker.

  4. 04

    Drink

    Straight away, while it is smooth.

Herbal drinks: 1 g sachet in 150 ml hot water.
