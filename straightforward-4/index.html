Questions people ask

Something else? Write to care@straightfrwd.com.

How much should I have in a day?

One serving a day. It is recommended as a one meal replacement, so have it in place of a meal rather than on top of three.

Is it whey protein?

No. The protein comes from soya protein isolate, pea protein and brown rice protein. It does contain sodium caseinate from milk.

Is it vegan or dairy-free?

No. It contains sodium caseinate, which comes from milk. It also contains soy.

Does it have sugar?

There is no added sugar. It is sweetened with sucralose and acesulfame potassium.

Sachet or 1 kg pouch?

Sachets are 50 g, one serving each, easy to carry. The 1 kg pouch gives you 20 servings for home.
