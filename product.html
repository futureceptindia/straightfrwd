    Product information

    What goes in, and why.

    Three protein sources

    Soya protein isolate as the base, with pea and brown rice protein covering the amino acids soya alone runs light on.

    Vitamins and minerals

    A full micronutrient premix, every vitamin and mineral named with its amount and %RDA on the back of the pack.

    Enzymes and fibre

    Amylase, protease, lipase, cellulase and lactase, plus FOS prebiotic fibre, so a large dose of plant protein goes down easier.

    One scoop, one serving

    The scoop ships in the pack. A 50 g serving goes into 250 ml of water, stirred until nothing sits at the bottom.

    Water, not milk

    The mix carries its own soy fat powder, so it goes creamy on water alone. Milk works, but adds calories the panel does not count.

    Where it is made

    Zeon Lifesciences in Paonta Sahib, Himachal Pradesh. The batch code on every pack traces back to the week it was made.
