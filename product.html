    How to make it

    Three steps, thirty seconds.

    1. Measure 50 g

      One serving, straight into the glass.

    2. 250 ml water

      Water, not milk. The mix carries its own fat powder, so it goes creamy on its own.

    3. Stir and drink

      Stir until nothing sits at the bottom, then drink it immediately after preparation.
