good questions

Asked often

Is this whey protein?

No. It is plant protein from soya protein isolate, pea protein and brown rice protein. It does contain sodium caseinate from milk, so it is not dairy-free.

How much should I have?

One 50 g serving a day. It is recommended as a one meal replacement.

Is there sugar in it?

No added sugar. It is sweetened with sucralose and acesulfame potassium.

Do the three flavours differ in nutrition?

No. Chocolate, Mango and Kulfi share the same formulation. Only the taste changes.

How many servings in a 1 kg pouch?

20 servings of 50 g each.

How do I make the herbal drinks?

Empty one 1 g sachet into 150 ml hot water and stir.
